The idea in one line

An income certificate that decides itself — and explains every decision.

Today a citizen applies, then waits in the dark. Our version reads the application, checks it against mock government registries, and does one of four things instantly: issues it, sends it for a quick human check, asks the citizen for one missing thing, or rejects it with a clear reason. A visible "statutory clock" counts the 3 working days the office is legally allowed — and if that time runs out, the app auto-drafts the appeal. Everything is explainable. Nothing silently gets stuck.

The split · two equal lanes

Lane A holds the brain and the data. Lane B holds everything people see and the demo.

Roughly the same amount of work each. They only touch through one shared set of TypeScript types (below), so you can both build at full speed in parallel. Codex (mandatory for this hackathon) is our engine co-pilot inside Lane A — it writes the core rules, a human reviews and integrates them.

Lane A

Engine & Data

The spine — decisions, deadlines, and every mock registry

You own how the system thinks: the rules that turn an application into a decision, the deadline math, and all the fake-but-realistic government data behind it.

  • 01
    Decision engine
    The 8 rules + scoring that map an application to one of 4 outcomes, with a bilingual reason for each. Lives in lib/engine. Codex drafts, you review & integrate.
  • 02
    SLA / deadline engine
    Working-day math, breach detection, ₹250/day penalty ledger, auto-drafted appeal on breach. lib/sla.
  • 03
    Data model + seed
    Prisma schema and the seed: 5 demo personas + 40 filler applications so the dashboard looks alive.
  • 04
    Mock registries
    5 fake government sources (Aadhaar-ish, Samagra, land, ration, tax) behind clean interfaces. All synthetic data.
  • 05
    API routes
    The endpoints the frontend calls — evaluate an application, advance the demo clock, fetch status.
  • 06
    Correctness tests
    Vitest suite proving each persona lands on the right outcome. This is our safety net for the demo.
Done when: feed any persona in → get the correct decision + deadline out, and the tests are green.

How we actually work

Four rules so two people move like one.

1
Types first, then split.

Nothing real gets built until the 5 shared types are frozen. That boundary is what lets us go parallel.

2
Your lane, your files.

Lane A lives in lib/ + api/. Lane B lives in the screens + app/. We rarely touch the same file.

3
Fake data is real work.

The mock registries and seed personas are the product in a demo. They must look convincing — but stay 100% synthetic.

4
The demo is the deadline.

Both lanes aim at one thing: 5 personas, running clean, on a phone screen, in front of judges.

Five things we never break

The non-negotiables — true for both lanes.

✕
All data is synthetic. No real Aadhaar, Samagra, or personal data anywhere. Aadhaar-like numbers are built to fail the real checksum so they can never collide with a real person's.
✕
Citizens never see a password or captcha. Phone number + OTP only. That's the whole login.
✕
Hindi is the default. English is a toggle, not the starting point.
✕
Works on a 360px phone. If it breaks on a small screen, it's broken.
✕
Nothing silently pends. Every automated decision has a reason a citizen can read.
